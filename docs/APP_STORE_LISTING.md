## Description (4000)

Relay is a messenger built so that only you and the people you talk to can read what you say.

Every message, photo, voice note and call is end-to-end encrypted with the Signal Protocol. Relay's servers pass along encrypted data they can't open, and they don't learn who you talk to.

NO PHONE NUMBER NEEDED
Sign up with Apple, Google, an email code, or just a passkey. Share a username, a link or a QR code instead of your number.

EVERYTHING YOU EXPECT FROM A MESSENGER
• Chats and groups with replies, reactions, edits, polls and stickers
• Voice and video calls, including group calls and call links
• Voice messages: hold to record, slide to cancel, listen back before sending
• Photos, videos and files, with in-chat search and a shared media page
• Live location for 15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours
• Spoilers, formatting, link previews built on your phone
• Message history that comes back when you sign in on a new phone (encrypted, optional)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Sealed sender: the server doesn't see who sent a message
• Disappearing and view-once messages
• Face ID lock for the app or for single chats
• Notification previews you control, down to "New message" only
• Safety numbers to verify who you're talking to
• An account password, passkeys and a recovery key so nobody can take over your account with your email alone
• No ads, no trackers, no selling data

MADE FOR iPHONE, iPAD AND APPLE WATCH
Home Screen widget, Control Center buttons, Siri and Shortcuts, Focus filters, CarPlay replies, Live Activities and a full iPad layout.

OPEN SOURCE
Relay's app and server code is public, so anyone can check how it works: github.com/CoderDude1232/Relay

Questions or feedback: support@relaymsg.app

## Age rating questionnaire

Unrestricted messaging between users, user-generated content and no
moderation of message content (it's end-to-end encrypted) put a messenger at
the platform's general-audience-with-communication rating (13+ under Apple's
2025 age ratings; Signal and WhatsApp sit at the same level). Answer "No" to
mature themes, gambling and so on; "Yes" to user-generated content and
messaging. Report and Block exist (see `APP_REVIEW.md`).

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Tracking: **No** (no data is used to track people across apps or websites).

Data Linked to You:
- **Contact Info → Email Address** (only if the user signs up with or adds an
  email; stored as a keyed hash, used for sign-in and account recovery) →
  App Functionality.
- **Identifiers → User ID** (account ID and username) → App Functionality.

Data Not Collected: message content, contacts (lookups are blinded on the
device), location (shared end-to-end encrypted, never readable by Relay),
photos, audio, browsing, usage data, diagnostics, purchases.

Double-check against the current privacy policy before submitting: the label
must match what the policy says.
