Where the encryption ends.

Every messenger has a boundary. Here's exactly where Relay's sits.

At your screen

Messages are decrypted on your device, so whoever can unlock it can read them.

At the other person

They can screenshot or repeat anything you send. No messenger changes that.

Not on our servers

Nothing readable, nothing kept — which is the part most apps get wrong.

Not readable in backups

Your history stays on your devices. Optional iCloud Backup is encrypted with a key only you hold.
