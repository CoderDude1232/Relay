Reading the table

WhatsApp and Signal are built on the same Signal Protocol Relay uses, and all three encrypt conversations end to end by default. The clearest difference from both is how you sign up: Relay needs no phone number, just a username and an email address.

"Deleted once delivered" means an encrypted message waits on our servers only until the recipient's phone collects it, or 14 days if it never does. Relay's optional backup goes to your own iCloud, encrypted with a recovery key only you hold. How Relay works covers the details.
